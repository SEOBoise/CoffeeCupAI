    Company profile

    Practical AI, with people accountable for the outcome.

    Coffee Cup AI is an artificial intelligence consulting business owned by Dan O’Donnell. The company helps leaders and teams evaluate where AI may be useful, how it fits existing workflows and what responsible implementation requires.

    Its published approach begins with business needs rather than a list of tools. It emphasizes measurable outcomes, reliable information, clear guardrails and human review.

    01 / Strategy

    AI Consulting

    Connect AI opportunities to workflows, priorities, people and measurable business outcomes.

    02 / Review

    AI Audits

    Review current tools, information quality, risks, constraints and possible next steps.

    03 / Education

    AI Speaking

    Keynotes, workshops and leadership discussions that make business AI understandable.
